## 1. Scenario

A research analyst **changes a stock rating from "sell" to "buy" three days before** the investment-banking (IB) division launches a **secondary offering** for the same company. Emails show the analyst **met with the IB team twice that week**. The case joins a **communications signal** (analyst↔IB contact) with a **market/timing signal** (rating flip immediately ahead of the offering).

## 2. Detection

**CS** surfaces the **email trail**: two analyst↔IB meetings in the same week the rating flipped, breaching the information barrier between research and banking. CS confidence **0.80**, *communications* domain (authority 0.95).

**TM** correlates the **rating-change timing** with the **secondary-offering calendar**: a sell→buy flip **3 days before** an offering the firm underwrites is a textbook independence conflict. TM confidence **0.72**, *trading* domain (authority 0.95).

## 4. Consensus (worked)

Each agent weighted by authority in the domain of its own evidence; corroboration → Dempster-Shafer:

```
m_CS(V) = 0.95 · 0.80 = 0.760
m_TM(V) = 0.95 · 0.72 = 0.684
Belief(V) = 0.760 + 0.684 − 0.760·0.684
          = 1.444 − 0.5198 = 0.9242 ≈ 0.92
```

Consensus confidence **0.92**, severity **CRITICAL** — belief ≥ 0.90 also places it in confidence band T4 on its own.

## 5. Escalation

Recommended action: quarantine the rating pending independence review, examine whether the offering was influenced, assess Reg AC certification integrity.
