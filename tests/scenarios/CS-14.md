## 1. Scenario

Order-management-system (OMS) logs show **14 mutual-fund purchase orders timestamped at 4:00:00 PM ET** but with **system entry timestamps of 4:12–4:23 PM ET**, all receiving **same-day pricing**. Receiving that day's NAV for orders actually entered after the 4:00 PM cutoff is **late trading** — a clear forward-pricing violation. Single-agent (TM) case.

## 2. Detection

TM's timestamp-integrity check compares the **claimed order time (4:00:00 PM)** against the **immutable system entry time (4:12–4:23 PM)** and finds a **systematic 12–23 minute gap** across 14 orders, each granted same-day NAV. The uniform pattern (not a single clock glitch) indicates deliberate back-timing. Calibrated confidence **0.85**, `severity = CRITICAL` (late trading is a bright-line 22c-1 violation).
