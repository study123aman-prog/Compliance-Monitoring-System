## 1. Scenario

A fund manager's portfolio reaches **28% concentration in a single sector** (prospectus limit **25%**) due to **mark-to-market appreciation and new purchases**, and the breach **persists for 5 trading days without corrective action**. A limit breach, not misconduct — single-agent (TM), MEDIUM severity.

## 2. Detection

TM's threshold monitoring compares live sector exposure against the **25% prospectus limit**, confirms the **28% breach persists 5 days**, and attributes the drift between **passive** (mark-to-market appreciation) and **active** (new purchases) causes — the active-purchase component is what elevates concern above pure market drift. Calibrated confidence **0.72**, `severity = MEDIUM` (a prospectus breach requiring corrective action; not a CRITICAL conduct issue).
