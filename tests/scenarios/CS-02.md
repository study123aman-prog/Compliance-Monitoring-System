## 1. Scenario

An algorithmic trading desk places large limit orders in crude-oil futures that are consistently cancelled within **200–500 ms**. Orders are placed on one side, followed by rapid execution on the *opposite* side — the classic spoofing/layering signature — occurring **47 times in a single session**. Only trading data is needed; this is a single-agent (TM) case.

## 2. Detection

TM's cross-market surveillance and temporal analysis fire on: (a) an abnormal **order-to-trade ratio**, (b) a **cancel-latency distribution** clustered in the 200–500 ms band, (c) **one-sided quoting pressure** immediately preceding opposite-side fills, repeated 47× intraday. These are strong, self-consistent structural signals, so TM's calibrated confidence is **0.85** with `severity = HIGH` (manipulative intent is inferred from pattern, not a smoking-gun communication — hence HIGH, not CRITICAL).
