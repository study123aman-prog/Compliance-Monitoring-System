## 1. Scenario

A trader consistently executes **personal-account trades 10–30 minutes before large client orders** in the same securities. Over three months, **89% of personal trades are profitable** with a **2.3% average return** — a win-rate that is statistically implausible without foreknowledge. Single-agent (TM) case.

## 2. Detection

TM's temporal analysis and counterparty analysis detect: (a) a **lead-lag pattern** (personal fills precede client orders by 10–30 min), (b) **same-security matching** between personal and client flow, (c) an **89% win-rate / 2.3% avg return** that is a statistical outlier vs the trader's risk profile. The consistency over three months makes this high-precision. Calibrated confidence **0.87**, `severity = CRITICAL`.
