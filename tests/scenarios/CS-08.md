## 1. Scenario

Marketing materials distributed to **3,400 prospects** claim **"guaranteed 12% annual returns"** and **"zero risk of capital loss"** for a structured product with an actual **40% capital-loss potential** in stress scenarios. The violation is in the communication itself; single-agent (CS) case, elevated to CRITICAL by breadth and the absolute-guarantee language.

## 2. Detection

CS's keyword/phrase and intent analysis flag prohibited constructions: **performance guarantees** ("guaranteed 12%"), **absolute risk denial** ("zero risk of capital loss"), and **omission of material downside** (40% loss potential). Materiality is high — **3,400 recipients**. Calibrated confidence **0.86**, `severity = CRITICAL` (misleading marketing at scale is a clear, high-precision violation).
