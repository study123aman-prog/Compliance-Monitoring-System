## 1. Scenario

Two accounts managed by **different portfolio managers** execute **34 matching trades** in thinly-traded corporate bonds over two weeks. The accounts **alternate as buyer and seller** with **identical quantities** and **prices within 2 basis points** — creating artificial volume with no real change in beneficial ownership. Single-agent (TM) trading-pattern case.

## 2. Detection

TM's counterparty analysis and cross-market surveillance detect: (a) a **matched-trade graph** between two accounts, (b) **buyer/seller role alternation**, (c) **identical size + sub-2bp price coincidence** (statistically implausible in a thin market), (d) **no net ownership change**. The thin liquidity amplifies the signal. Calibrated confidence **0.82**, `severity = HIGH` (wash-trading pattern is strong but intent/coordination across two PMs benefits from human confirmation — hence HIGH).
