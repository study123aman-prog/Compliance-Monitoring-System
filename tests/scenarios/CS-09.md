## 1. Scenario

A corporate client's **wire transfer is routed through three intermediary banks** to a **final beneficiary that is a subsidiary of an OFAC SDN-list entity, added 48 hours ago**. The violation depends on two facts owned by different agents: the **transaction routing** (TM) and the **freshness of the sanctions listing** (RU). This is why both are weighted in the *sanctions/AML* domain, not one dominant author.

## 2. Detection

**TM** reconstructs the payment chain (originator → 3 intermediaries → beneficiary), matches the beneficiary against the enriched sanctions graph, and identifies the **indirect SDN linkage** (subsidiary of a listed entity). TM confidence **0.82**, evaluated in the *sanctions* domain (authority 0.70).

**RU** confirms the beneficiary's parent was **added to the SDN list 48 hours ago** — a very recent change TM's static lists might have missed — and supplies the citation. RU confidence **0.92**, *sanctions* domain (authority 0.75). RU's recency is what pushes the case over the line.

## 4. Consensus (worked)

Both opinions sit in the **sanctions/AML domain**; corroboration path → Dempster-Shafer, `mᵢ(V) = wᵢ(sanctions)·cᵢ`:

```
m_TM(V) = 0.70 · 0.82 = 0.574
m_RU(V) = 0.75 · 0.92 = 0.690
Belief(V) = 0.574 + 0.690 − 0.574·0.690
          = 1.264 − 0.3961 = 0.8679 ≈ 0.87
```

Consensus confidence **0.87**, severity **CRITICAL**. (Even though each single weight is modest, corroboration across two independent sanctions signals yields high combined belief — the intended behaviour of the combination rule.)

## 5. Escalation

CRITICAL → severity floor **T4**; confidence 0.87 → band T3; **tier = max(T4, T3) = T4** (Director/CCO). **Sanctions special trigger** independently forces T4 and an **immediate transaction hold** (automated protective control — holding a payment is reversible; releasing a sanctioned payment is not). SLA ack 15 m / resolve 24 h. Human authorises any OFAC filing (dual control per filing tree).

## 6. Report & audit

RG drafts an OFAC/SAR-track sanctions report with the payment chain, the listing citation, and the hold record. All steps hash-chained/signed under `correlation_id = C9-SANC`.
