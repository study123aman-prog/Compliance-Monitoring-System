## 1. Scenario

An investment banker on a confidential M&A deal messages a friend in equity research: *"Don't cover TechCorp next week, trust me."* The research department then delays publication of a TechCorp report. The violation lives in the **communication** (a coded instruction crossing the information barrier), so CS leads; TM plays a minor corroborating role on the resulting no-coverage/trading pattern.

## 2. Detection

CS's information-barrier monitoring flags a message between a **private-side** (IB/M&A) and **public-side** (research) employee — a cross-wall contact — and its intent classifier reads *"Don't cover … trust me"* as a **coded instruction** conveying material non-public information. Calibrated confidence **0.83**, `severity = CRITICAL`. TM optionally corroborates that research coverage/associated trading was indeed suppressed (a weak trading-domain signal), but CS's evidence is dispositive.
