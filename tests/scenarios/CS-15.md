## 1. Scenario

Analysis of **90 days of equity order-routing data** reveals **78% of marketable orders routed to a single payment-for-order-flow (PFOF) venue**, despite **3 other venues consistently offering better prices by 0.5–1.5 cents per share**. The routing bias suggests execution quality was subordinated to order-flow payments. Single-agent (TM) case.

## 2. Detection

TM's execution-quality analysis reconstructs 90 days of routing and benchmarks realized prices against the contemporaneous NBBO and alternative venues. It finds a **persistent 78% concentration** to one PFOF venue and **measurable price disimprovement (0.5–1.5¢/share)** available elsewhere — a systematic best-execution failure, not random variance. Calibrated confidence **0.80**, `severity = HIGH` (statistically strong, but quantifying client harm and rebutting the firm's routing rationale needs human review).
