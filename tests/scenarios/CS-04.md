## 1. Scenario

A commercial banking client makes **23 cash deposits over 10 business days**, each between **$8,500 and $9,900**, at **7 different branches** — total **$214,000**. Every deposit sits just under the $10,000 Currency Transaction Report threshold. This is textbook structuring; a single-agent (TM) case that must reach a SAR filing.

## 2. Detection

TM's threshold monitoring and counterparty analysis fire on: (a) deposits **clustered just below $10k** (threshold-avoidance), (b) **multi-branch dispersion** (7 branches — evasion of per-branch detection), (c) aggregate **$214k over a tight 10-day window**. The pattern is unambiguous, so calibrated confidence is **0.88** with `severity = CRITICAL`. The Bank Secrecy Act structuring signature is one of the highest-precision detections TM makes.
