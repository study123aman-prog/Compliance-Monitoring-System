## 1. Scenario

The SEC publishes a **final rule increasing initial margin for uncleared swaps by 25%**, effective in **120 days**, with new variation-margin methodologies that **differ from Basel III** standards. This is not a violation but a **regulatory change** the firm must absorb — RU's core mandate: detect, assess impact, extract the timeline, and flag a cross-regulation conflict.

## 2. Detection

RU's feed monitoring ingests the Federal Register publication. It runs: (a) **impact assessment** (policy/procedure/system gap analysis — margin models, collateral systems), (b) **timeline extraction** with calendar alerts at **180/90/60/30/14 days** before the 120-day effective date, (c) a **cross-regulation conflict** note where the new VM methodology diverges from Basel III CRE54 / EMIR. Preliminary assessment confidence **0.70**, `severity = MEDIUM` (a change requiring action, not an incident). Per its constraints, RU's assessment is **preliminary and requires human validation** — it cannot interpret ambiguous language or modify rules itself.

## 3. Message trail

RU **broadcasts** an `UPDATE` to all agents (recalibrate margin-related thresholds) and sends an `ALERT` to the Orchestrator to open an impact-assessment case.

## 5. Escalation

MEDIUM → severity floor **T1**; confidence 0.70 → band **T2**; **tier = max(T1, T2) = T2** (Senior Analyst) to own the impact assessment. SLA ack 4 h / resolve 5 business days. The 180/90/60/30/14-day alerts are scheduled so deadlines cannot silently lapse. Human validation of the preliminary assessment is required before any rule change is applied.
